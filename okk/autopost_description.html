Introduction

Autopost is a comprehensive AI-powered social media scheduling and management platform. It lets you plan, create, and publish content across multiple social networks from one dashboard. Connect Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube, LinkedIn, Twitter (X), and TikTok; schedule posts with a visual calendar; run campaigns; and use AI to generate captions and content. Autopost supports flexible subscription plans (monthly and yearly), multiple payment gateways, role-based panels for Super Admin, Admin, and User, and a customizable landing page. With media galleries, templates, hashtag management, post analytics, and optional two-factor authentication, Autopost simplifies social media management for teams and businesses.

Features

AI-Powered Scheduling: Plan and publish posts across social networks with optional AI-generated content and captions.

Multi-Platform Support: Connect and post to Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube, LinkedIn, Twitter (X), and TikTok from one dashboard.

Visual Calendar: Schedule posts with a calendar view, drag-to-reschedule, and publish-now options.

Campaign Management: Create campaigns with start/end dates, target multiple accounts, and manage content and media in one place.

Subscription & Billing: Flexible monthly and yearly plans with Stripe and PayPal integration; optional PostSaaS addon for full pricing and billing.

Multiple Payment Gateways: Support for Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, Instamojo, Mollie, Paystack, SSLCommerz, Mercadopago, Flutterwave, and more.

Role-Based Access: Separate panels for Super Admin (settings, gateways, languages, maintenance), Admin (posts, campaigns, social accounts, AI), and User (profile, dashboard).

Social Login: One-click login via Google and Facebook for faster registration.

Media & Video Galleries: Centralized image and video uploads for use in posts and campaigns.

Templates & Hashtags: Reusable post templates and hashtag management for consistent branding.

Post History & Insights: Track published posts and fetch engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares) from supported platforms.

Retry & Cancel: Retry failed posts or cancel pending scheduled posts from the dashboard.

Multiple Storage Options: Store media on local disk or cloud (AWS S3, Wasabi, Vultr, DigitalOcean).

Security: Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), reCAPTCHA, and email/SMS verification (Twilio) for secure access.

Cookie Consent: GDPR-compliant cookie consent using Spatie Laravel Cookie Consent.

Backup & Maintenance: Automated backups (Spatie Laravel Backup) and maintenance mode for safe updates.

Multi-Language & Translation: Built for easy translation and multi-language support.

Support Tickets (Addon): Optional PostSaaS addon includes ticket system and subscription refund requests.

Analytics & Reports: Dashboard analytics and reporting for posts and activity.

Mobile Responsive: Fully responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
